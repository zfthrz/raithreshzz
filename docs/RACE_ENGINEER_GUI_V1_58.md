# Race Engineer GUI v1.58

GUI v1.58 improves use at reduced window widths without changing application data.

- The summary uses four columns at wide widths.
- It becomes a two-by-two card grid at medium widths.
- Cards and visual previews stack vertically at narrow widths.
- The session catalogue can be collapsed from the workspace header.
- `Ctrl+B` toggles the catalogue without changing the selected session.
- The existing plan inspector remains independently closable with its button or Esc.

The responsive rules change widget placement and scroll extent only. They do not
modify telemetry, coaching, History, H3 automation, calibration or validation.
